Overview — CoastalPulse Ghana PUBLIC PROTOTYPE

The problem: Keta's coastal communities face erosion, flooding and environmental-information gaps.

The solution: CoastalPulse Ghana is building a repeatable coastal intelligence system — combining satellite, environmental and community data to understand changing coastal risk across hazards: erosion, flooding, water quality and plastic pollution.

What works today: interactive Keta/Kedzi map · satellite-derived shoreline-change analysis (2017–2025, 106 transects, indicative) · indicative historical flood-extent layer for one Keta event (Nov 2021) — the first entry in a planned multi-event flood library, not a one-off map · community-reporting interface (interface prototype, no backend) · environmental-risk dashboard (illustrative concept, not measured).

What funding enables next: local community co-design · GPS field validation · expanding the historical flood-event library · water-quality field monitoring · plastic-pollution mapping · community reporting backend · environmental sensors where justified.

What this prototype does NOT contain

  • No live sensors or sensor feeds of any kind.
  • No AI / machine-learning analysis — none is built or implied.
  • No community-submitted data — the reporting form is an interface mockup; the demo log is illustrative.
  • No field measurements by Street Pulse Foundation. Satellite-derived shoreline change and flood extent are computed from open archives (indicative, not survey-grade — see Methods); ground-truth field validation is still pending.
  • No flood-depth estimates and no validated flood extents — the flood layer is indicative co-event water extent versus normal-condition extent.
  • No claim of community participation, partnership, or willingness anywhere. "Proposed pilot location" wording is used throughout.
