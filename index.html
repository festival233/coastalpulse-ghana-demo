Community reporting INTERFACE PROTOTYPE

This form demonstrates how community members could one day submit coastal observations. No backend exists. Submissions are kept only in this browser session and are labeled DEMONSTRATION DATA. Live reporting begins with the pilot launch — not before.

Risk / intelligence dashboard DEMONSTRATION

Illustrative per-zone risk scores for the Keta coastal strip. Scores are not measured; they are assigned from documented hazard facts (see Methods) to demonstrate the dashboard format. Field validation is pending.

What this prototype does NOT contain

  • No live sensors or sensor feeds of any kind.
  • No AI / machine-learning analysis — none is built or implied.
  • No community-submitted data — the reporting form is an interface mockup; the demo log is illustrative.
  • No field measurements by Street Pulse Foundation. Satellite-derived shoreline change is computed from open archives (indicative, not survey-grade — see Methods); ground-truth field validation is still pending.
  • No claim of community participation, partnership, or willingness anywhere. "Proposed pilot location" wording is used throughout.
