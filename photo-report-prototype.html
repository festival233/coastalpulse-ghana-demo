Who is reporting?

No name needed. Just pick the role that fits best. Names and phone numbers are never asked for in the field.

Before you send

Please read this. It is written in plain language on purpose.

What happens to your report:

  • Your report, photo and location go to the Street Pulse Foundation review team. Nothing is published automatically.
  • A reviewer checks it first. Only reports the team verifies can ever appear on a public map — without your name.
  • Faces policy: if your photo shows people's faces, reviewers blur faces before anything is published. Photos with identifiable people are not published without permission.
  • What we strip: the photo's hidden camera data (serial numbers, device info) is wiped on your phone. We keep only the photo, its GPS position and the date.
  • What we never ask for: your name, phone number, address or ID.
  • You can ask for your report to be removed at any time.

      Sync protocol & conflict policy (design)
      
    
        
  • Ordering: oldest first. Text/metadata uploads before photos; photos oldest first.
    • 
        
  • Retry with backoff (demo-shortened): attempts wait 3s → 8s → 20s here. Production schedule: 1 min → 5 min → 30 min → 2 h → 12 h, then a gentle banner ("Some reports haven't sent — check your signal"). Nothing is ever deleted without the user's explicit confirmation.
    • 
        
  • Deduplication: every report carries a client-generated dedup key (clientId + content hash). Retried uploads reuse the key, so a retry can never create a duplicate on the server.
    • 
        
  • Conflict policy — server wins, flagged for admin review: if the server already holds a different version of a report (e.g. edited by a reviewer), the local copy is not silently overwritten and not force-pushed. It is marked conflict, kept locally, and flagged for admin review with both versions visible.
    • 
        
  • Idempotency is a backend contract: the server must accept the same dedup key twice without creating two reports (workstream C).
    • 
        
  • 7-day rule: items older than 7 days and still unsent trigger the gentle banner — never auto-delete.
    • 
      

    

    

      What this prototype implements for real vs. simulates
      
    
        
  • Real: EXIF GPS extraction from the actual photo file (hand-written JPEG/APP1/TIFF parser, no library); client-side compression (canvas re-encode, max 1600px, JPEG q0.7) with before/after byte counts; EXIF stripping by re-encode (privacy by construction); form validation against the backend schema rules.
    • 
        
  • Simulated: network (the toggle), upload latency/failures, server responses, conflict injection, GPS "simulate fix" button. The outbox is in-memory JS, not IndexedDB; there is no service worker in this single-file demo.
    • 
        
  • Design only: service worker caching strategy, real IndexedDB schema, Background Sync, voice notes, Ewe/Ga localization, reviewer/admin flow.
